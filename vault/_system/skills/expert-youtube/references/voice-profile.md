# Voice profile template

Lives as `youtube-voice.md` in the user's profile folder. Every workflow reads this file first. On YouTube the words are spoken out loud, so a script written in the wrong voice cannot be read on camera.

**How to fill it:** Ask the questions one at a time, or draft it from three video transcripts the user gives you. **Show the file to the user and get their approval before writing it.** No personal data (address, phone, ID) goes in.

```markdown
# YouTube voice profile

## Who I talk to
One person, with their situation. Not "students" but "a third-year student who finished a first data science project but does not trust it enough to put it on GitHub".

## How I really talk
A few paragraphs from the transcript of your best-performing video. The voice you have, not the one you wish you had.

## Words I never use
The ones that are not yours, concretely: "epic", "mind-blowing", "in this video you'll learn everything".

## Words I do use
Your own phrases and habits. They are kept.

## What I will not claim
Numbers you cannot show, results that are not yours, tools you have not used. Write them down so the skills refuse them for you.

## My format
Length, how the opening goes, whether there is an intro, whether you use slang or swear, whether you appear on camera.
