# Turkish spelling rules (summary)

This file is a Turkish spelling reference, used when the vault language is Turkish. The rules and examples are about Turkish; the Turkish examples are kept as they are.

Source: the Turkish Language Association (TDK) spelling rule pages, read on 6 November 2026. A summary in our own words. Wrong examples marked "(inference)" were derived from the rule and are not on the page.

| Rule | Explanation | Right / wrong | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conjunction "da/de" | Written separately, follows vowel harmony, never "ta/te", not attached with an apostrophe | R: "Ayşe de geldi." W: "Ayşe'de geldi." | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/baglac-olan-da-denin-yazilisi/ |
| Locative suffix "-da/-de/-ta/-te" | Written attached to the word | R: "yolda kalmak" W: "yol da kalmak" (inference) | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/bulunma-durumu-eki-da-de-ta-tenin-yazilisi/ |
| Conjunction "ki" | Written separately; fixed forms such as "belki, çünkü, sanki" are attached | R: "demek ki", "belki" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/baglac-olan-kinin-yazilisi/ |
| Question particle "mi" | Written separately, follows vowel harmony; later suffixes attach to it | R: "Kaldı mı?", "Verecek misin?" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/soru-eki-mi-mi-mu-munun-yazilisi/ |
| Capital letters | Start of a sentence; names of people, places, languages, institutions | R: "Mustafa Kemal Atatürk" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/buyuk-harflerin-kullanildigi-yerler/ |
| Attached compound word | Those with a dropped sound or a shift in meaning are attached | R: "pazartesi", "kahvehane" W: "kahve hane" (inference) | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/bitisik-yazilan-birlesik-kelimeler/ |
| Separate compound word | If the second word keeps its meaning, separate | R: "duvar saati", "kara yolu" W: "duvarsaati" (inference) | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/ayri-yazilan-birlesik-kelimeler/ |
| Numbers | A thousands period from four digits up; a decimal comma; no space between "%" and the number | R: "1.250.000", "15,2", "%25" W: "% 25" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/sayilarin-yazilisi/ |
| Ordinal numbers | Written with digits, either a period or an apostrophe and suffix; never both | R: "8'inci" or "8." W: "8.'inci" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/sayilarin-yazilisi/ |
| Comma | Between words and phrases of equal function in a series | R: "Umduk, bekledik, düşündük." | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/noktalama-isaretleri-aciklamalar/ |
| Colon | At the end of a sentence that is followed by an explanation or an example | R: "Kendimi tanıtayım: …" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/noktalama-isaretleri-aciklamalar/ |
| Semicolon | Separates groups that have commas inside | R: "Türkiye, İngiltere; Ankara, Londra." | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/noktalama-isaretleri-aciklamalar/ |
| Apostrophe | An inflectional suffix on a proper noun is separated by an apostrophe | R: "Fatih Sultan Mehmet'e" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/yazim-kurallari/noktalama-isaretleri-aciklamalar/ |
| Circumflex (^) | Not abolished; where it is used is in the guide | R: the distinction "kâr / kar", "hâlâ / hala" | https://tdk.gov.tr/icerik/sikca-sorulan-sorular/duzeltme-isareti-kaldirildi-mi/ |

The current and full guide: `yazim.tdk.gov.tr` (not opened while preparing this summary). When unsure, look at the guide; do not make up a rule.
